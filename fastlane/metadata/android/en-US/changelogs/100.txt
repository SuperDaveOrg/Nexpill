The first release of Nexpill as an Android app, replacing the web version. Track doses for everyone you care for: interval, fixed-time, as-needed and taper schedules. Reminders are exact alarms held by the phone, with Mark given and Snooze on the notification, and an alarm mode for doses that can't be missed. Corrections, dose history as CSV, a summary for the doctor, and backups to a file. No account, no internet permission.
